## Facts used (all from the HP document)
- 25 clinical specialists at HGZ No. 20 "La Margarita" (IMSS) and the ISSSTE Regional Hospital, Puebla, July 2–28, 2026.
- Pulmonology, HGZ 20: the pathway takes about a year and a half; nearly all cases arrive at stage IV.
- Nursing: "I feel sick, but I'll go tomorrow because I have to work; otherwise, what will my family eat?"
- "A system that fails people, not a lack of education among them."
- Responsible design: triage, not a verdict; questionnaire at the 1st level, test ordered at the 2nd level (Dr. Arely Pineda); result with next steps, emotional support and family (focus groups 2 and 3).
- Outro: "NEUMA means breath, and this is not our final one… in lung cancer every hour counts."
